## Findings and verification

Priorities: **P0** immediate exposure or critical failure; **P1** launch-blocking for the agreed scope; **P2** improvement that can be scheduled. Severity depends on context, not a tool's raw score.

| ID | Priority | What / why it matters | Status and evidence | Action: fix now or user action | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [ID] | [P0/P1/P2] | [what; why] | [PASS/FAIL/UNVERIFIED/NOT RUN/N/A + evidence] | [concrete action] | [owner] |

`PASS` requires supporting evidence; `FAIL` means evidence contradicts the requirement; `UNVERIFIED` means the check was attempted but evidence was insufficient; `NOT RUN` means a known prerequisite was unavailable; `N/A` needs a reason. Distinguish proposed, implemented, and verified changes in the next-action field.

Keep the founder-facing response to the decision summary and the highest-priority bullets. Put exhaustive evidence, source URLs, query tables, and low-priority notes in an appendix or separate saved report. Keep trademark/name notes to 2–3 lines unless a deeper review was requested.
